Market position

AiSignal is not another observability platform.

Datadog, Dynatrace, New Relic, and Splunk are powerful enterprise observability platforms. AiSignal is designed for teams that already run an open-source observability stack and want AI-powered diagnosis without replacing it.

Not the target customer

Organizations that already standardized on full-suite enterprise observability platforms may already receive telemetry collection, dashboards, alerting, APM, service maps, security monitoring, digital experience monitoring, and cloud cost analytics in one commercial platform.

  • They may already be deeply invested in Datadog, Dynatrace, New Relic, or Splunk.
  • Their core problem is not tooling availability.
  • They may not want another layer unless it adds strong explainability or institutional memory.

The AiSignal target customer

Teams that intentionally use open-source observability want to keep licensing costs low while still improving incident understanding and reporting.

  • They already run Prometheus, Loki, Tempo, and Grafana.
  • They pay $0 in core observability licensing fees.
  • They need faster RCA, better reports, and less manual dashboard correlation.

Alert-driven intelligence

AiSignal can subscribe to Grafana and Prometheus alerts.

AiSignal should not only generate reports on demand. It can listen to existing alert streams and automatically create an evidence-backed incident report when a service crosses a meaningful threshold.

Subscribe to existing alerting

Most OpenTelemetry-first teams already have alert rules in Prometheus Alertmanager or Grafana Alerting. AiSignal can receive those alerts through webhook integrations and use them as the trigger for deeper analysis.

  • Prometheus Alertmanager webhook integration.
  • Grafana Alerting contact point integration.
  • Service, severity, labels, annotations, and time window passed into AiSignal.
  • AiSignal expands the alert into logs, traces, metrics, deployment context, and likely root cause.

Set AiSignal-specific alert policies

AiSignal can also define its own lightweight intelligence rules for signals that traditional alerts may miss, such as exception-pattern spikes, trace-error concentration, or deployment-linked regression patterns.

  • Run cheap health scans across all registered services.
  • Generate AI reports only when something is worth explaining.
  • Avoid token waste by separating deterministic scans from AI diagnosis.
  • Create incident briefs, postmortem drafts, or daily health summaries depending on state.

Processing pipeline

Eight steps from raw signal to operational answer.

The goal is not just summarization. The goal is structured reasoning over evidence.

01–02

Collect signals

Query metrics from Prometheus and extract exception-heavy logs from Loki.

03–04

Expand traces

Retrieve trace IDs from Tempo and expand traces into spans and dependencies.

05–06

Correlate evidence

Join spans, logs, errors, and timestamps to identify the likely failure path.

07–08

Generate report

Prepare structured input and produce a human-readable diagnosis with actions.
